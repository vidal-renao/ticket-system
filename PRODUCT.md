# Product

## Vision

Provide Swiss SMEs with a focused, multilingual support workspace that makes requests traceable, keeps response commitments visible and assists staff without removing human review.

## Users

- Customers need a simple way to request help and understand progress.
- Agents need a prioritized queue, context and efficient response tools.
- Managers need workload and SLA visibility.
- Administrators need controlled user and organization management.

## Product Principles

1. A ticket always has a clear owner, status and next action.
2. AI suggests; people approve customer-facing communication.
3. Privacy and tenant isolation take precedence over convenience.
4. Operational states must be understandable without training.
5. Metrics must be derived from real data and state their limitations.

## Scope

Current scope includes ticketing, comments, assignment, SLA, notifications, analytics, multilingual UI and AI assistance. Payment processing, enterprise SSO, external chat integrations and certified legal compliance are outside the implemented scope.
